Quick rule of thumb

For a full-time job (40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours a year), double the hourly wage and add three zeros to estimate the yearly salary. $20 an hour ≈ $40,000 a year (exactly $41,600).

Unpaid time off?

If you don't get paid vacation, lower "paid weeks per year." For example, 2 unpaid weeks off means 50 paid weeks.

FAQ

How much is $20 an hour per year?

Working 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, $20 an hour is $41,600 a year before taxes.

How many work hours are in a year?

A full-time year is usually 2,080 hours: 40 hours a week × 52 weeks.

How do I convert salary to hourly?

Divide the yearly salary by the hours you work in a year. $50,000 ÷ 2,080 hours ≈ $24.04 an hour.
