How to thaw a turkey safely

  • In the fridge (easiest): about 1 day for every 4–5 lb. Keep it in its wrapper on a tray. Once thawed, it keeps 1–2 days in the fridge.
  • In cold water (faster): about 30 minutes per pound. Keep it wrapped, fully covered in cold water, and change the water every 30 minutes. Cook it right away.
  • Never thaw a turkey on the counter at room temperature.

FAQ

How much turkey do I need per person?

Plan about 1 pound of whole turkey per person. If you want leftovers, plan about 1½ pounds per person.

How long does it take to thaw a turkey?

In the refrigerator, allow about 24 hours for every 4 to 5 pounds, so a 16-pound turkey needs about 4 days. In cold water, allow about 30 minutes per pound and change the water every 30 minutes.

How long do I cook a turkey?

At 325°F, an unstuffed 12 to 14 pound turkey takes about 3 to 3¾ hours. Bigger birds take longer, and stuffed turkeys take a little longer again. Always check with a food thermometer.

How do I know my turkey is done?

Use a food thermometer. It's done when the thickest part of the breast, the innermost part of the thigh and the wing all read at least 165°F. If it's stuffed, the center of the stuffing must also reach 165°F.

Can I cook a turkey from frozen?

Yes, it's safe, but it takes at least 50% longer than a thawed turkey, and you can't stuff it.

Times and safety tips are based on USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service guidance. Ovens vary, so always check with a food thermometer.
