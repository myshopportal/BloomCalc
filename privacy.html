Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

What you type into our tools

Our calculators run in your browser. The numbers and names you enter are not sent to us or stored on our servers. Some tools, like the gift budget planner, save your entries in your own browser's local storage so you can come back to them. You can delete them any time with the tool's "Start over" button or by clearing your browser data.

Hosting and basic logs

Our website host may automatically record basic technical information, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site secure and working.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, to understand how many people visit and which tools are popular. These tools may use cookies and collect information like your device type and approximate location.

Advertising and affiliate links

BloomCalc contains affiliate links, including links to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. When you click an affiliate link, the store may use cookies to know that you came from BloomCalc.

We may show ads from Google AdSense or other ad networks. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Children

BloomCalc is a general-audience website and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

Our pages link to other websites, like stores and ShopPortal. Their privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
