How long to cook a turkey

Quick answer

At 325°F, an unstuffed turkey takes about 3–3¾ hours for 12–14 lb and 3¾–4¼ hours for 14–18 lb. Stuffed turkeys take a bit longer. It's done at 165°F in the thickest part.

Tips for a juicy turkey

  • Start checking the temperature at the shorter time.
  • It's done at 165°F in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost thigh and the wing. See where to check the temperature.
  • Let it rest 20 minutes before carving so the juices settle.
  • Cooking from frozen? Allow at least 50% more time.

FAQ

How long to cook a 20 lb turkey?

At 325°F, an unstuffed 20 lb turkey takes about 4¼–4½ hours, or 4½–5 hours if it's on the 20–24 lb end. Stuffed, allow about 4¼–4¾ hours.

How many minutes per pound for turkey?

Roughly 13–15 minutes per pound for an unstuffed turkey at 325°F, but the weight chart is more accurate, and a thermometer is the only sure test.

Should I cook my turkey at 350°F instead?

You can roast at 325°F or higher. The USDA chart times are for 325°F. A hotter oven cooks faster, so check the temperature earlier.

Food safety times and temperatures are from USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service guidance. Ovens vary, so always check with a food thermometer.
