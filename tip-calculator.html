Quick US tipping guide

Customs vary by place and service. Some restaurants already add a service charge for big groups, so check the bill before adding a tip.

Fast mental math for a 20% tip

Move the decimal one place left to get 10%, then double it. On an $86.40 bill: 10% is $8.64, so 20% is about $17.28.

FAQ

How much should I tip at a restaurant?

In the US, 15–20% of the bill before tax is common for sit-down service, and many people tip 20% or more for great service.

Should I tip on the total before or after tax?

Tipping on the amount before tax is the traditional way. Tipping on the total after tax is also common and just a little more generous.

How do I split a bill with a tip?

Add the tip to the bill, then divide the total by the number of people. This calculator does both and can round each share up to a whole dollar.
