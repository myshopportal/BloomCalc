FAQ

How is exact age calculated?

We count full years since your birth date, then the full months since your last birthday, then the days left over. That's the same way age is counted on forms.

What if I was born on February 29?

In years without a February 29, we count your birthday as February 28. Some people celebrate on March 1 instead.

Is my birth date saved?

No. The calculation happens in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored.
