About business days

Business days here are Monday to Friday. Public holidays aren't removed, so subtract any holidays in your range yourself.

FAQ

How do I count the days between two dates?

Enter both dates above. By default we count the days in between without the end date, like most date calculators. Tick "include the end date" to count both days.

What date is 30 days from today?

Use the "add or subtract days" tool. Today's date is filled in for you, so just enter 30.

Are holidays counted as business days?

Yes, this calculator counts all Mondays to Fridays. Remove public holidays yourself if they fall in your range.
