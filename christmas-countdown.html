FAQ

How many days until Christmas?

The countdown at the top of this page updates live, every second. Christmas Day is December 25 every year.

What day of the week is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 is on a Friday, and Christmas Eve is on Thursday, December 24.

When is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thanksgiving in the US is the fourth Thursday of November. In 2026 that's Thursday, November 26. Black Friday is November 27 and Cyber Monday is November 30.

How many sleeps until Christmas?

Sleeps are the number of nights left before Christmas morning. The countdown shows it under the main timer.
