How long to thaw a turkey

Quick answer

In the fridge, allow about 1 day for every 4–5 pounds, so a 16 lb turkey needs about 4 days. In cold water, allow about 30 minutes per pound and change the water every 30 minutes.

Turkey thawing chart

WeightIn the fridgeIn cold water
4–12 lb1–3 days2–6 hours
12–16 lb3–4 days6–8 hours
16–20 lb4–5 days8–10 hours
20–24 lb5–6 days10–12 hours

Fridge method (easiest)

  • Keep the turkey in its wrapper and set it on a tray to catch drips.
  • Your fridge should be 40°F or colder.
  • Once thawed, the turkey keeps 1–2 more days in the fridge.

Cold water method (faster)

  • Keep the turkey in a leak-proof bag so water can't get in.
  • Fully cover it with cold tap water, and change the water every 30 minutes.
  • Cook it right away after thawing.

Never do this

Don't thaw a turkey on the counter or in hot water. The outside warms up to unsafe temperatures while the inside is still frozen.

FAQ

How long to thaw a 20 lb turkey?

About 4–5 days in the fridge, or about 10 hours in cold water.

How long to thaw a 12 lb turkey?

About 3 days in the fridge, or about 6 hours in cold water.

Can I refreeze a thawed turkey?

A turkey thawed in the fridge can be refrozen, though it may lose some quality. A turkey thawed in cold water should be cooked right away, not refrozen.

Food safety times and temperatures are from USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service guidance. Ovens vary, so always check with a food thermometer.
