Tips to stay on budget

  • Set the total first, then divide it. It's much easier than adding up as you go.
  • Use tiers: a bigger amount for close family, a fixed small amount (like $15) for friends, teachers and coworkers.
  • Don't forget the extras: wrapping paper, cards and shipping can add up to 10% more.
  • Track every buy right away, even small ones.
  • Try a family gift swap (Secret Santa) if your list is long.

FAQ

How much should I spend on Christmas gifts?

There's no right number. Start with what you can pay without debt, then split it by person. Many people set a bigger amount for close family and a small fixed amount for friends, teachers and coworkers.

How do I stick to my gift budget?

Write your list before you shop, give each person a set amount, and update what you spent as you buy. Shopping sales like Black Friday helps your money go further.

Is my list saved?
