About BloomCalc

Free, simple calculators for the questions that come up in everyday life.

BloomCalc was made by Aleena, the creator behind ShopPortal and Aleena Finds. The idea is simple: when you need a quick answer, like how big a turkey to buy or how many days until Christmas, you should get it in seconds, on your phone, without ads jumping around or sign-ups.

How our tools work

  • Every calculation runs right in your browser. Your numbers aren't sent to us.
  • Where it matters, we use trusted sources. For example, our turkey times come from USDA food safety guidance.
  • Results are estimates to help you plan. For health, money or legal decisions, check with a professional.

How BloomCalc is free

Some pages include affiliate links. If you buy through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. As an Amazon Associate, BloomCalc earns from qualifying purchases. We may also show ads in the future.
