When to start thawing your turkey for Thanksgiving 2026

Quick answer

For Thanksgiving on Thursday, November 26, 2026, move your turkey to the fridge by: Monday, Nov 23 (up to 12 lb), Sunday, Nov 22 (12–16 lb), Saturday, Nov 21 (16–20 lb), or Friday, Nov 20 (20–24 lb).

Thaw-start dates for Thanksgiving 2026

Turkey weightDays in fridgeMove to fridge by
Up to 12 lbup to 3 daysMon, Nov 23
12–16 lb3–4 daysSun, Nov 22
16–20 lb4–5 daysSat, Nov 21
20–24 lb5–6 daysFri, Nov 20

These dates use the longer end of the USDA fridge-thawing range, so your turkey is fully thawed by Thanksgiving morning. A thawed turkey keeps 1–2 days in the fridge, so a day early is fine.

Forgot to start in time?

  • Cold water: about 30 minutes per pound, changing the water every 30 minutes. A 16 lb turkey takes about 8 hours.
  • Cook it frozen: it's safe, but takes at least 50% longer. See how to cook a frozen turkey.

FAQ

When should I take my turkey out of the freezer for Thanksgiving 2026?

Move it to the fridge by Monday, Nov 23 for a turkey up to 12 lb, Sunday, Nov 22 for 12–16 lb, Saturday, Nov 21 for 16–20 lb, or Friday, Nov 20 for 20–24 lb.

What day is Thanksgiving 2026?

Thanksgiving 2026 is Thursday, November 26.

Can I thaw my turkey too early?

A turkey thawed in the fridge keeps 1–2 days after it's fully thawed, so starting a day early is fine. Much earlier than that, it's better to wait.

Food safety times and temperatures are from USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service guidance. Ovens vary, so always check with a food thermometer.
